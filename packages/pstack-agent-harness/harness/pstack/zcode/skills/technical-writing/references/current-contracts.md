# Keep prose complete and current

Read this when shortening instructions, updating public docs, writing an upgrade guide or reconciling decisions. Work within the requested scope; a prose audit alone does not authorize edits or a repository-wide history cleanup.

Before editing a passage, identify its actor, action, condition, timing, modality, negative guarantee, exception, owner, effect and failure consequence. Shorten only when those relevant facts survive. Keep the necessary local contract and link deeper rationale to one owning page. A word count is not a correctness test.

Describe shipped behavior in current docs. Replace private design-session labels and review choreography with a resolvable owner or a standalone fact. Keep incident chronology and historical evidence in their appropriate records. Do not erase a lasting safety rationale merely because it describes a past failure.

Update the source before generated catalogs or snapshots, and follow their real consumers. For bilingual changes, update the affected counterpart in the same change and compare meaning and terminology. A matching hash or paired-file presence does not prove translation quality. Prompt, diagnostic and UI wording is behavior and needs its owning evidence, not only a prose check.

For an externally observable break, state the affected release/surface, old and new behavior, ordered migration steps, verification and recovery. Reuse the existing install or migration guide. Internal changes whose consumers move together do not require a new release-note system.

When a decision is superseded, compare its unique rationale, compatibility obligations and inbound links before consolidating it. Retain partial supersessions and meaningful rejected alternatives with their reintroduction conditions. Frozen historical records stay historical. Do not import another project's archive layout, deletion policy or thousands of notes. Memory writes and deletion still require the repository's actual authorization.
