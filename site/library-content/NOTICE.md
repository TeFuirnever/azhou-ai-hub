# Source Library first edition

Anthropic released the formal seven-chapter text of [Claude’s Constitution](https://www.anthropic.com/constitution) under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Amanda Askell is the primary author. The archive includes Overview through Concluding thoughts. The text was extracted on 2026-11-26 from the publisher’s page, dropping the webpage shell, preface, acknowledgments and expansion controls. The [edition data](first-edition.json) records the original response hash, approved archive revision and body file hash. To refresh, recheck the license on the publisher’s page and compare the seven chapter headings and normalized text before updating the body and hashes.

The other five records carry their original title, URL, date and Azhou’s own reading notes.

The [approved edition](first-edition.json) records the release timestamp. The public site and RSS feeds are built from this edition.
